Answer truthfully. For this app: no violence, no sexual content, no gambling, no drugs. The app lets users send

## 5. Things to check on a real phone before publishing

The app is the website, so almost everything is the same. Check these on an Android phone with the app installed:

- [ ] Log in, and stay logged in after closing and reopening the app.
- [ ] The Festival library loads quickly and the pictures show.
- [ ] Pick a card, send it, and the campaign progress bar moves.
- [ ] Upload a logo from the phone gallery and a contacts file.
- [ ] Download a card (it saves to the phone) and share to WhatsApp.
- [ ] Notifications arrive (a new reply, a finished campaign).
- [ ] **Connect your WhatsApp number from inside the app.** Meta's connect window is a popup and may not open inside
      the app. If it does not, the page shows a link to open it in Chrome; connect there and return.
- [ ] Buying a plan: the payment page opens and returns to the app.
- [ ] No address bar shows (if one does, the app link is not verified; see below).

**App link check:** the site already serves `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` for `shop.sendkar.twa`. After the new
bundle is uploaded, Google Play App Signing re-signs it with its own key. Add that key's SHA-256 fingerprint (Play
Console, Setup, App signing) to `assetlinks.json` next to the existing one, or the address bar will appear.

## 6. Release steps (only on your instruction)

1. Raise `appVersionCode` to 2 and `appVersionName` to 1.1.0 in `android-app/twa-manifest.json`.
2. Update the in-app name to the new short name.
3. Build the release bundle (needs the signing key in `android-app/`).
4. Upload to **Internal testing** first. Install it on your own phone and run the checklist above.
5. If your developer account is a **personal** one created recently, Google requires a closed test with at least 12
   testers for 14 days before production. An **organisation** account does not. Check which yours is.
6. Fill in the store listing, data safety and content rating from this document, then roll out to production.
