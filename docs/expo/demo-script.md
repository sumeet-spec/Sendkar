# Tech Expo Gujarat: stand plan and 3-minute demo

27 to 29 November 2026. Built around one idea a shopkeeper understands in ten seconds:

> "Diwali is coming. Send a greeting with your own name and logo to every customer on WhatsApp, in their language, in two minutes."

Keep it to that. Do not explain the product. Show it.

## Before the expo (do these in order)

| When | What | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 weeks before | Connect the Sendkar SIM, send a message to yourself | Everything else depends on a working number |
| 2 weeks before | Submit the starter templates and one **image-header** template to Meta | Approval can take days, and the image header has never been tried with Meta |
| 10 days before | Switch on signup verification (set `SIGNUP_OTP_REQUIRED=true`) once the system number works | So signing up on the spot proves the number |
| 1 week before | Print the counter poster (Gujarati) and the flyers in `docs/expo/` | Printing takes time; check the QR scans |
| 1 week before | Build a demo account: brand kit for a sample shop, 20 to 30 of your own and friends' numbers as contacts | A believable list to send to |
| 3 days before | Run the whole demo twice on the stand's Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot | Venue Wi-Fi is the most likely failure |
| Day before | Record the demo on screen as a backup; save screenshots of the sent card on a phone | If the internet fails, you still have something to show |

Never send a demo to real customers' numbers. Only to numbers whose owners agreed.

## The 3-minute demo

Say the words in the shopkeeper's language. Ask their shop name first and use it.

1. **The problem (20 seconds).** "Diwali is in a few days. How do you tell all your customers?" Let them answer. Most say phone calls or a WhatsApp broadcast to 256 people.
2. **The library (30 seconds).** Open Festival library, language Gujarati. "Every festival, a new design, free, before the festival." Pick one. Type their shop name into the brand kit while talking. Show their name on the design.
3. **Add an offer (20 seconds).** Type "15% છૂટ, રવિવાર સુધી". The card updates.
4. **Send (40 seconds).** Press Send this to my customers, pick the template, press Send now. Show the progress bar filling and the phone in your hand receiving the card. This is the moment.
5. **Permission (30 seconds).** Hand them the counter poster. "Your customers scan this and agree themselves." Scan it with your phone, enter a number, show the count go up.
6. **Close (20 seconds).** "7 days free. Put your number in now and I will set you up." Sign them up on the spot.

## Words to use and avoid

- Say: festival card, your customers, your name and logo, free for 7 days, in their own language.
- Do not say: broadcast, API, automation, CRM, platform. Do not name other products.
- Do not lead with price. If asked: plans start by number of WhatsApp numbers (1, 5, 10), and Meta charges a small fee per message separately, about as published on their price list.

## Questions you will get

- **"Will my number get blocked?"** Customers who say yes themselves, the counter QR, and an automatic stop if too many messages fail all protect the number.
- **"Do I need a new number?"** No. The same number can be used for business, and personal chats stay private.
- **"I do not know English."** The whole app, and the cards, are in Gujarati, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada.
- **"What if I have 5,000 customers?"** It sends in minutes. A new number starts with a smaller daily limit set by WhatsApp, and that limit grows as the number is used well.

## If something fails

- No internet: play the screen recording, show the phone screenshots.
- Template not approved yet: show the card in the library and the campaign page; say "WhatsApp approves the message once, then it is reusable".
- Number not connected at the stand: do the demo up to the campaign page, send a test to your own phone from a second device that is connected.

## Leads

Collect a name, shop type and WhatsApp number from everyone who stops. Follow up the next morning with a festival card made with **their** shop name. That message is the best sales pitch.
